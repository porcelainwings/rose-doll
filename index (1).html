Story

Two records of one life

What Rose remembers is soft and broken. What the lab wrote down is neither.

The forest

Rose was born a Veena in the forests at the foot of the Skatay Range, about a hundred and ten years ago. She was given a forest name at birth, like every Viera. She doesn't remember it.

Sometimes a smell of rain on leaves, or the hush under tall trees, makes her stop walking. She can't say why.

Seraph Project · AcquisitionRef. XX-0417

Following the Dalmasca campaign, units swept the surrounding forests to collect Viera. Veena subjects taken from the Skatay foothills. Long-lived and naturally aether-attuned. Suitable.

Objective: a living field healer able to channel concentrated Light-aspected aether into wounded soldiers. Method adapted from Allagan records on solar aether collection, Crystal Tower.

Infusion log · Subject "Doll"Year 6 of 25

Light saturation continues. Skin, hair and eyes fully depigmented. Wing structures began forming in year four, failed, and settled into dermal markings. Markings conduct aether. Retained.

Memory loss total. Subject no longer responds to forest name. Daily stabilizing tonic (Skatay flower distillate) prevents full transformation. Side effect: persistent floral scent.

I've started calling her Rose, for the scent. "Doll" is cruel. — initials

Rose, and Doll

For twenty-five years she was Doll: posed, examined, and put back on the shelf. One researcher took pity on her and gave her something prettier. When she finally got out, she kept both names. Rose is the kindness she was shown. Doll is what she was made into.

To the people she heals, "Doll" means something you hold onto for comfort. To her, it's the truest word for how she feels: emptied out and filled with too much Light.

Facility statusFinal entry

Garlemald has fallen. Funding ceased. Staff dispersing. Subject unaccounted for. Data removed from site by persons unknown.

Now

Rose wanders, heals who she can, and doesn't ask how anyone got their wounds. People who've been to the First sometimes go quiet when they see her. She looks a little like the Sin Eaters there: same cause, similar result, but she has never set foot on the First. She doesn't understand why she bothers them.

"You don't have to tell me what you did. Just let me hold it for a while."

The gift

Touch, sight and feathers

Healing by touch

Her Light-aspected aether flows into others through contact. A hand on the brow eases pain. An embrace mends real wounds. The deepest healing needs real closeness, though it doesn't have to be sexual.

Through touch she also feels what someone carries, grief, guilt, the weight of past choices, and draws some of it off.

Aether-sight

Her eyes see aether instead of the world. She lost the Wood's voice in the lab, but she can see its light in every living thing, except herself. Her own reflection is colorless.

The feathers

The wing and feather markings are what remains of the wings she never fully grew. The wings gather and store Light. The scattered feathers carry it down her arms to her hands, and they glow brighter as she heals.

What it costs her

  • She has to be close. No healing at range, and crowds and distances are hard to navigate.
  • Brightness hurts. Glare, radiant magic and Light-aspected places wash out her sight and cause real pain.
  • Healing drains her. Big wounds exhaust her, and she briefly feels the pain or grief she took.
  • Slower than conjury. Deeper, but a trained conjurer closes a wound faster.
  • Too much Light. Without darkness nearby she grows restless and overbright.
  • No memory. Fragments surface through smells, sounds or Garlean magitek, often at bad moments.

Heart

Who she is

At home in the dark

Her aether leans so far toward Light that darkness steadies her. Monsters, shadowed places and dark-hearted people don't frighten her. They comfort her, and she feels through their vivid colors what she can't find in herself.

She doesn't fix people

She has seen what Light does when it tries to perfect something. She won't try to make anyone into something they're not. She helps the morally grey carry what they are and eases their regrets, without judging how they got there.

Simple on the surface

Amnesia and years of isolation left gaps in ordinary manners. She touches freely and can seem naive, even gullible. Her empathy runs deep, though. She often notices she's overstepped only after touching someone and feeling their mood shift, then gently pulls back.

Companion

Halo

She found him wounded, driven off by black ravens who wouldn't accept a pale one among them. She healed him, and he never left.

He glides and hovers just above her head, keeping watch. He sees what she can't at a distance and guides her through bright or crowded places.

An angel without wings or a halo: the markings stand in for the wings, and the raven is her halo.

OOC only: the truth behind her

Everything in the Story section is out-of-character knowledge. In character, Rose knows almost none of it, and other characters would have to uncover it through play. Please don't metagame the lab files.

  • The kind researcherThe one who named her Rose may still be alive, as an ally, a source of answers, or someone still torn.
  • Other subjectsShe may not have been the only one.
